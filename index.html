    LEARNING OVER TIME

    Feedback makes the next forecast more informed.

    Over several comparable decisions, teams can assess recurring optimism and whether uncertainty ranges reflect actual outcomes.

    One missed forecast does not establish bias. Deal complexity, market conditions and execution can all explain differences.

    What a pilot would measure

    Forecast error, unresolved assumptions, useful challenge prompts, false positives and added review time.

    THE PRODUCT CONCEPT

    Behavioral finance inside investment approvals.

    DecisionCheck would add independent forecasts, benchmark challenges, premortems and outcome feedback to existing financial analysis. Its proposed customers include CFOs, corporate development teams and investment committees.

    What makes the concept specific

    The differentiation is the capital-allocation workflow and committee record. Other tools, including DecideIQ, already advertise bias detection. This prototype uses transparent calculations and review prompts rather than AI diagnoses.

    Research behind the approach

    • Malmendier & Tate (2008): CEO overconfidence and acquisitions
    • McKinsey: Biases in decision-making, a guide for CFOs
    • McKinsey: Premortems
